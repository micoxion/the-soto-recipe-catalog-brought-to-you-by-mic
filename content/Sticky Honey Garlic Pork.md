# Ingredients
### For the Pork
- [ ] 1 lb (450g) pork tenderloin or pork shoulder, thinly sliced into strips
- [ ] 3 tablespoons cornstarch
- [ ] 2 tablespoons vegetable oil (for frying)
### For the Sauce
- [ ] 1/4 cup soy sauce
- [ ] 1/4 cup honey
- [ ] 4 cloves garlic, minced
- [ ] 1 tablespoon rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- [ ] 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger (optional)
- [ ] 1/2 teaspoon red chili flakes or 1 small fresh red chili, sliced (optional for heat)
### For Garnish
- [ ] 2 green onions, chopped
### For serving
- [ ] Cooked white rice
# Directions
#### 1. Prepare the Pork:
Start by placing the sliced pork into a bowl. Sprinkle the cornstarch over the pork and toss it well until all the pieces are evenly coated. This step is key for getting that delightful crispy texture when we fry it!
#### 2. Frying the Pork:
Heat the vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high heat. Once the oil is hot, carefully add the coated pork strips in a single layer. Fry them for about 4-5 minutes, or until they are golden brown and cooked through. When done, remove the pork from the pan and set it aside on a plate.
#### 3. Making the Sauce:
In the same pan, reduce the heat to medium. If the pan looks dry, feel free to add a splash of oil. Now, add the minced garlic and grated ginger if you’re using it. Sauté everything together for about 30 seconds until it starts to smell amazing!
#### 4. Combine Sauce Ingredients:
Next, stir in the soy sauce, honey, and vinegar. Mix everything well and let the sauce come to a gentle simmer. This is where all the flavors start coming together!
#### 5. Tossing the Pork:
After the sauce is simmering, add the fried pork strips back into the pan. Toss everything together so that the pork is coated nicely in that sticky sauce. Cook for an additional 2-3 minutes to let the sauce thicken up and cling to the pork.
#### 6. Add Spice (Optional):
If you want a little heat in your dish, now’s the time to sprinkle in the red chili flakes or add the fresh chili slices. Stir them in to combine all the spicy goodness!
#### 7. Garnishing the Dish:
Once the sauce has thickened and the pork is nicely coated, remove the pan from the heat. Garnish your sticky honey garlic pork with the chopped green onions for a fresh pop of color and flavor.
#### 8. Serve and Enjoy:
Serve your sticky honey garlic pork immediately over warm, cooked white rice. Enjoy every sticky, sweet, and savory bite!

Enjoy your Sticky Honey Garlic Pork! It’s sure to become a favorite meal in no time!
