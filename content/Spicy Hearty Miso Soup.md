# Ingredients
- [ ] 2 tbsp. olive or vegetable oil
- [ ] 2 Potatoes
- [ ] 1 Onion
- [ ] 2 Carrots
- [ ] 4 cloves of Garlic
- [ ] 1 tsp grated ginger
- [ ] 1 tbsp sesame oil
- [ ] Green onions to taste
- [ ] 4 tbsp. Miso
- [ ] Tobanjan/Gochujang fermented chili paste to taste
- [ ] 4 cups of water
# Directions
1. Cut vegetables into a bite-size, no larger than 3/4 in
2. In a pot in heated oil sauté’ onions until they become translucent; then add the other vegetables, grated ginger, and garlic and sauté’ 3 more minutes.
3. Add water and boil until all the vegetables are soft; reduce heat to simmer and add sesame oil, and fermented chili paste. Finally add the Miso steadily ensuring it is mixed in without clumps
4. Bring to a simmer and garnish with chopped leeks or sliced green onions.
