# Ingredients
### Cauliflower Steaks
- [ ] 2 1/2 cups (300 g), all-purpose flour
- [ ] 1 tsp. Kosher salt
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 1 large egg
- [ ] 3/4 cup buttermilk
- [ ] 1/4 pickle brine
- [ ] 2 Tbsp. Vinegar-based hot sauce (such as Frank's RedHot)
- [ ] 2 small heads of cauliflower, leaves and stem trimmed
### Spice Blend
- [ ] 1 Tbsp. Cayenne pepper
- [ ] 2 tsp. Garlic powder
- [ ] 2 tsp. Onion powder
- [ ] 2 tsp. Smoked paprika
- [ ] 1/2 tsp. Kosher salt
- [ ] 1/4 tsp. Freshly ground black pepper
### Kale Slaw
- [ ] 1/2 cup mayonnaise 
- [ ] 1 tbsp. Apple cider vinnegar
- [ ] 1 tbsp. Honey
- [ ] Kosher salt
- [ ] Freshly ground black pepper
- [ ] 2 cups packed coarsely chopped stemmed kale leaves (about 2 oz.)
- [ ] 2 cups tricolor coleslaw (about 8 oz.)
### Dave's Sauce
- [ ] 2 chipotle chiles in adobo, finely chopped, plus 3 Tbsp. adobo sauce
- [ ] 3/4 cup mayonnaise
- [ ] 2 Tbsp. Honey
- [ ] 1/4 tsp. Garlic powder
- [ ] Kosher salt
### Assembly
- [ ] Vegetable oil, for frying (4-5 cups)
- [ ] 1 tbsp, light or dark brown sugar
- [ ] 4 potato rolls, toasted
- [ ] 12 dill pickle chips

# Directions
### Cauliflower Steaks
1. In a shallow bowl, whisk flour, paprika, salt, and a few grinds of black pepper. In another shallow bowl, whisk egg, buttermilk, pickle brine, hot sauce, and 1/2 tsp. salt.
2. Arrange cauliflower on a cutting board stem side down. Cut lengthwise into 2 (1/2"-thick) “steaks.” You should have 4 steaks total.
3. Dip each steak into egg mixture, letting excess drip off. Dredge in flour mixture, pressing firmly to adhere. Repeat the process once more. Arrange on a wire rack set in a baking sheet. Let sit 30 minutes.
### SPICE BLEND
1. In a small bowl, mix cayenne, garlic powder, onion powder, paprika, salt, and black pepper.
### KALE SLAW
1. In a large bowl, whisk mayonnaise, vinegar, and honey; season with salt and pepper. Add kale and coleslaw and toss to coat; season with salt and pepper, if needed. Refrigerate until ready to use.
2. **Make Ahead:** Slaw can be made 3 days ahead. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate.
### DAVE'S SAUCE
1. In a medium bowl, combine chiles, adobo sauce, mayonnaise, honey, and garlic powder; season with salt. Refrigerate until ready to use.
2. **Make Ahead:** Sauce can be made 5 days ahead. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate.
### ASSEMBLY
1. Set another wire rack in another baking sheet. Into a large, heavy skillet, pour oil to a depth of 1/2". Heat over medium heat until an instant-read or deep-fry thermometer registers 350°. Working in batches if needed, fry breaded cauliflower, turning halfway through, until deep golden brown, 3 to 4 minutes per side. Transfer cauliflower to prepared rack to drain. Remove skillet from heat.
2. Transfer 1 Tbsp. spice blend to a medium heatproof bowl. Stir in brown sugar. Carefully spoon 1/2 cup hot oil over spices (they should sizzle) and stir to combine. Dip each steak into seasoned oil and return to wire rack. Season steaks all over with remaining spice blend. 
3. Spread sauce on bottom buns, then top with slaw, pickles, and hot cauliflower. Drizzle more sauce over steaks and close up sandwiches with top buns. Serve with remaining sauce alongside for dipping.

> [!note] Mic's Notes
> I'd recommend making some of this ahead of time like the sauce and kale slaw. I felt like the prep was a bit long when done all at once. This does make quite a bit of batter so don't be scared to really coat the cauliflower. I really enjoyed it though, the flavor was good and its got some substantial heat.
