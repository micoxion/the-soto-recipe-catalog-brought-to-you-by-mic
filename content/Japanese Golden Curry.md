# Ingredients
- [ ] 1 lb chicken (chopped)
- [ ] 1 large white onion (minced)
- [ ] 1 medium potato (chopped)
- [ ] 1/2 medium carrot (chopped)
- [ ] 1 Tbsp Vegetable Oil
- [ ] 540 ml water
- [ ] S&B Golden Curry Sauce Mix 92g, 1 pack
# Directions
1. Stir-fry meat and vegetables with oil in a large skillet on medium heat for approx. 5 min.
2. Add water and bring to boil. Reduce heat, cover and simmer until ingredients are tender, approx. 15min.
3. Turn the heat off, break S&B Golden Curry Sauce Mix into pieces and add them to the skillet. Stir until sauce mixes are completely melted. Simmer approx. 5 min., stirring constantly.
4. Serve hot over rice or noodles.

# Modifications/Notes
> [!quote]+ Tylor
> Some unsolicited advice from your friendly neighborhood chef BIL, try searing your chicken off in batches next time! Crowding the pan will drop its heat, and all the released juices will create a simmering pool rather than steaming off. Less moisture = more Maillard reaction, which is a fancy way of saying “better sear.” You’re basically caramelizing the proteins and carbohydrates within the meat this way, and developing some good brown bits in the pan. Once you’ve done that, you place the chicken to the side and add your onions and carrots to the pan, and deglaze the pan with the liquid your onions render. Scrape up all those good brown bits from the chicken, and you’ll get even more flavor into your curry.

Basically, cook the meat first, giving it a good sear, then remove chicken, cook veggies, and combine at the end. If you give extra time to the chicken to build up a nice caramelized coating on the outside this really comes through in the flavor of the final product.
